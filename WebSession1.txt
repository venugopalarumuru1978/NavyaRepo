NetWork :-  An inter-connection b/w two / more than two computers to share the information. 

3 types of networks 

1. Local Area Network (LAN) :- here computers are connected with cables. It will be done within the building for 				offices.

2. Metro Politon Area Network (MAN) :- here computers are connected within city to city. here network will be establised using Satilites. 		
				Ex:-  TV Channels

3. Wide Area Network(WAN) :-  here computers are connected one location to another location in the entire world. 
				Ex:-  Internet
