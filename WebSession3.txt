------------

	<tr>
		<th>Col-Head-1</th>
		<th>Col-Head-2</th>
			..............
			..............
		<th>Col-Head-n</th>
	</tr>

	<tr>
		<td>Col-Val-1</td>
		<td>Col-Val-2</td>
			-------------
			-------------
		<td>Col-Val-n</td>
	</tr>

	...................
	...................
</table>

tr - table row
th - table head (col headings)
td - table data (col value)
